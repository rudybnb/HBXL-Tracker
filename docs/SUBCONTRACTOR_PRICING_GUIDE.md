# SUBCONTRACTOR PRICING GUIDE

**Room-Based | Labour-Only | Item-Complete**
*(With First Fix & Second Fix Explained)*

---

## 1. HOW THIS PRICING SYSTEM WORKS (PLEASE READ)

This project uses a **room-based, item-complete pricing system**.

- You are **not** pricing construction phases.
- You are **not** pricing percentages or stages.
- You **are** pricing **labour only**, per room, per individual item.
- Each item is paid **only when that item is fully complete**.

---

## 2. WHAT "PER ROOM" MEANS

A **room** is the main commercial unit.

Examples:
- Bathroom
- Kitchen
- Lounge
- Bedroom

Each room contains a list of clearly defined **labour items** relevant to that room.

You may price:
- Only the rooms you are invited to price
- Only the items relevant to your trade

---

## 3. FIRST FIX & SECOND FIX — IMPORTANT CLARIFICATION

**First Fix and Second Fix are shown for clarity only.**

They are used to:
- Explain sequencing
- Help you understand when work happens
- Help with programme planning

**They are not payment stages.**

### 🔑 Golden Rule

> **Payment is made per completed item, not per fix stage.**

---

## 4. HOW FIRST FIX & SECOND FIX ARE USED IN THIS SYSTEM

Inside each room, items are grouped under:
- **First Fix** (Informational)
- **Second Fix** (Informational)

These are **labels only**.

Each line item:
- Is priced separately
- Is completed separately
- Is paid separately

---

---

## 6. WHAT YOU ARE PRICING (LABOUR ONLY)

**You are pricing:**
- Installation labour
- Fixing and fitting work
- Your time and skill

**You are NOT pricing:**
- Materials
- Fixtures supplied by others
- Design changes
- Provisional allowances

Materials are excluded unless clearly stated otherwise.

---

## 7. HOW AND WHEN YOU GET PAID

Payment is released when an **individual item** is complete.

Examples:
- First fix electrics (Bathroom) complete → **paid**
- Door fitting complete → **paid**
- 5 sockets installed → **paid for 5 sockets**

There are:
- ❌ No percentage payments
- ❌ No "first fix complete" payments
- ❌ No stage valuations

---

## 8. WHAT YOU CANNOT CHANGE

To keep pricing consistent and fair:

**You cannot:**
- Change quantities
- Combine items
- Add materials
- Add contingency
- Convert items into lump-sum phases

If something appears missing or unclear:
➡️ **Raise a clarification before submitting your tender.**

---

## 9. OPTIONAL: ROOM LABOUR LUMP SUM (IF OFFERED)

In some cases, you may see an option to price a full room labour package.

Example:
> Bathroom – Full labour package: £X,XXX

If used:
- The system will internally split this back into items
- Item-complete payment rules still apply
- QS approval is required

**Default position: price per item.**

---

✔ Clear scope per room
✔ Clear sequencing (first / second fix shown)
✔ Clear payment triggers
✔ Faster certification
✔ No valuation disputes
✔ No percentage arguments

**You price what you do.**
**You complete the item.**
**You get paid for that item.**
